 Key Design Answer

 Your intuition about the hybrid model is correct: knowledge graph + vector DB. The graph gives deterministic multi-hop paths and provenance; the vector index gives semantic seed retrieval. That's
 exactly what build-world-model.py and the Wikipedia coprocessor implement.

 The cost comparison in retro-v2-review.md and the proposal doc highlights the core difference: RETRO's upfront cost is embedding trillions of tokens + PB-scale NVMe; OCTO's cost is domain
 curation + small structured graphs.
