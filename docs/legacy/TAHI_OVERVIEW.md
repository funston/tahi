## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

**TAHI (Optimal Co-processor Topology Architecture)** is an enterprise-grade Knowledge Graph co-processor system designed to solve the critical limitations of standard Vector Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Vector RAG).

While Vector RAG relies on black-box probabilistic cosine similarities over unstructured text chunks, TAHI extracts, canonicalizes, and indexes high-density Property Graphs. This delivers:
1. **1-Click Deterministic Provenance**: Every extracted relation carries an explicit `source_chunk_id` metadata tag, enabling exact sentence-level traceability.
2. **Instant Fact Revocation (<1ms)**: Invalid or outdated facts can be deleted instantly via Cypher/Kùzu queries without needing to re-chunk or re-embed the entire corpus.
3. **Multi-Hop Relational Traversal**: Enables LLMs to reason across complex entity relationships that span multiple non-adjacent document chunks.

---

## 2. Core Problem & The TAHI Thesis

### The Failure Modes of Vector RAG
- **Fragmented Context**: When a complex question requires synthesizing facts from Chunk #2 and Chunk #150, standard top-$k$ vector search frequently retrieves one chunk while missing the other.
- **Hallucination & Black-Box Provenance**: Cosine distance in embedding space cannot explain *why* a chunk was retrieved or *which* sentence supports a generated claim.
- **Costly Data Maintenance**: Deleting or updating a single fact in a vector database requires re-embedding large volumes of text.

### The TAHI Thesis Under Test (from [`TAHI_PLAN.md`](file:///home/rich/share/work/tahi/TAHI_PLAN.md))
> *"A structured Property Graph lets an LLM answer questions that require combining facts which are not co-located in any single passage, better than dense vector retrieval over the same corpus."*

## 3. Current System Status & Empirical Baseline Metrics

All figures below are drawn directly from machine-generated JSON artifacts in `data/graphrag_bench/`:

- **Corpus Processing & Build Cost**: Processed 199 1200-token chunks (1.06 MB corpus) in 3,285 seconds ($0.259 per MB) using `gpt-4o-mini` with gleaning enabled.
- **Graph Yield & Canonicalization**:
  - **4,379 Nodes** & **1,078 Canonical Relation Types**.
  - **5,224 Directed Triples** (collapsing to **4,800 Unique Undirected Edges** due to 424 multi-edges between identical node pairs).
  - Relation canonicalization successfully merged 8 misspelled metastasis variants (`metastazises_to`, `can_metastasize_to`, etc.) into `metastasizes_to` (116 edges). Recorded in `manifest.json`.
- **Graph Topology**:
  - **Giant Component**: 3,146 nodes (**71.8%** of total graph).
  - **Leaf Nodes**: 72.69% of nodes are degree-1 endpoints.
- **Path Connectivity Diagnostic**:
  - Evaluated 44,044 gold entity pairs across 509 Complex Reasoning questions.
  - **76.56% of gold entity pairs** are connected by multi-hop paths of length $L \ge 2$ ($L=2$: 16.46%, $L=3$: 22.65%, $L \ge 4$: 37.45%, $L=1$: 5.32%, Unconnected: 18.12%).
- **Step 2 Stratified Evidence Recall ($N=100$)**:
  - Fact Retrieval: **38.38%**
  - Complex Reasoning: **36.67%**
  - Creative Generation: **34.73%**
  - Contextual Summarize: **8.44%**
  - **Overall Stratified**: **29.55%**
